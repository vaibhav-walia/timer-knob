Timer Knob

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Timer Knob doesn't collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

On your device. The app saves the timer's state and your last set time on your iPhone, so a running timer survives the app closing. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications. If you allow them, the app schedules a notification on your iPhone for when a timer ends. These are created on your device and aren't sent through any server.

No tracking. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party services, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Changes. If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.
